# Gym and core catalog (cycling and running)

A general exercise bank. Translate exercise names, load cues, and rest cues into the rider's language when building the session. The aim is durable force and stability, not mass. Once or twice a week, never the day before aerobic-ceiling or threshold work, a long run, or a speed session. Riders and runners 50+ keep two sessions a week year-round. Adjust to the rider: more posterior chain for power, more single-leg and core work for stability.

## Strength (legs and posterior chain)

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Load / cue | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| Back squat (or trap-bar deadlift) | 4 × 5 | heavy, full control, smooth depth | 2–3 min |
| Romanian deadlift | 3 × 8 | posterior chain, hinge from the hips | 90 s |
| Bulgarian split squat | 3 × 8 / leg | dumbbells, balance and single-leg force | 90 s |
| Step-up | 3 × 8 / leg | controlled up and down | 90 s |
| Calf raise | 3 × 12 | slow, full range | 60 s |
| Hip thrust | 3 × 8 | glute drive, short pause at the top | 90 s |

## Running additions (impact resilience)

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Load / cue | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single-leg calf raise (straight and bent knee) | 3 × 12 / leg | slow lowering, full range | 60 s |
| Lateral band walk | 2 × 15 / side | hips level, knees out | 45 s |
| Single-leg deadlift | 3 × 8 / leg | hinge, hips square | 90 s |
| Pogo hops | 3 × 20 | stiff ankles, quiet landing | 60 s |
| Box jump or drop landing | 3 × 5 | stick the landing (advanced, not in race week) | 90 s |

Plyometrics count as a high-intensity leg session: never on consecutive days with hill reps, speed work, or heavy squats.

## Core (trunk and stability)

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Load / cue | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plank | 3 × 45 s | brace hard, hold the riding position | 45 s |
| Side plank | 3 × 30–45 s / side | hips high | 45 s |
| Pallof press | 3 × 10 / side | anti-rotation, resist the cable | 45 s |
| Dead bug | 3 × 10 / side | low back to the floor | 45 s |
| Bird dog | 3 × 10 / side | slow, no hip wobble | 45 s |

## Building a session

- Full strength session: 2–3 strength lifts + 2 core moves.
- Core only: 3–4 core moves.
- Warm up 5–10 min before loading. Progress by load on the compound lifts, not by adding reps.
- In-season (Build, Peak): one session, 2 lifts at maintenance load; drop plyometrics in the taper; nothing heavy in race week.
- Off-season (Transition, Base): two sessions, progressive load, this is where strength is actually built.
- Put each chosen exercise in the gym table from `schedule-format.md`: name, sets × reps, load/cue, rest.

# Heat training (core-temperature sensor)

For a cyclist with a core-temperature sensor (CORE or similar). A heat session is an ordinary easy endurance ride in a
hot room: it adds no length and no load to the plan, and the ride keeps its endurance targets. Numbers mirror
`assets/rules.json` `heat`.

## Who and when

- Only a cyclist who has the sensor, and only on an easy endurance ride of **50–90 minutes** that is not outdoor-only
  (indoor first). Never a recovery spin, a key, a long ride, a test or a race.
- Never the day before a key, long, test or race day, never the day after a race, and never in the **7 days** before an
  A race.
- No block for a plan that starts without a base under it (comeback, off-season, start running).

## A block, then maintenance

Adaptation needs consecutive exposure, so heat comes as a block:

- **Initial block:** the first **2** load weeks of general or base from the plan's **second** load week (never week 0,
  week 1, a recovery or transition week), up to **4** sessions a week, consecutive days allowed. It needs at least
  **5** sessions; if two weeks cannot hold them, extend to **3** weeks, and if three still cannot, skip the block.
- **Maintenance:** after the block, **one a week** in general, base, build and peak; none in recovery, transition,
  taper or race week. Never on consecutive days.
- Twelve days without heat lose most of the adaptation (about 95 % down to about 42 %); re-acclimation takes about four
  sessions instead of ten.
- A pre-race heat block (10 sessions in 14 days ending 3–6 days before a hot race) is a real protocol but needs the
  event to be known as hot; not planned yet.

## The session

- **Setup:** indoors, no fan, one extra non-breathable layer, a heater or a closed room, bottles ready.
- **Warm-up:** 15 minutes easy until core reaches **38.5 °C**.
- **Zone:** hold **38.5–39.0 °C** for **40 minutes** at the same effort; at **39.0 °C** ease off or open a window.
- **Limit:** no more than **75 minutes** in the heat; then fan on and finish easy.
- **Stop:** at **39.5 °C**, or at once if dizzy, sick, chilled or with a headache.
- **Effort:** endurance power or RPE 3–4; heart rate drifts to about **75–80 % of max** (lower in a hotter, more humid
  room).
- **Drink:** to sweat rate, usually **1–2 litres an hour**, with a strong electrolyte mix; weigh before and after and
  replace one and a half times the loss over the next hours.
- **Cool-down:** 10 minutes easy with the fan on.
- **Skip it** when ill, after a bad night, or the day after another heat session outside a block. A block is extra
  load: keep the rest of the week easy.
